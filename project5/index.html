Hello! My name is Sehui Yoo.

I am an undergraduate, multi-faceted artist studying at Syracuse University. I have been making art for around 14 years and am majoring in Studio Arts.

With my strong foundation in Fine Art and growing focus on communication design, I am interested in creating work that bridges human connection and digital media. I aspire to continue strengthening my creative practice through projects that challenge me in both the traditional and digital world.
